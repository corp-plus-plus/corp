# Corp++ Corporate Code of Conduct

## Our Executive Pledge
We are committed to providing a harassment-free, high-synergy, and mutually aligned collaboration environment for all participants regardless of corporate title, level, compensation bracket, or sprint velocity.

## Expected Standards of Alignment
- Treat all cross-functional partners with professional courtesy.
- Accept constructive feedback with grace and actionable action items.
- Focus on what is best for the collective enterprise mission.
- Protect shared core competencies.

## Violations & Disciplinary Action
Unacceptable behavior may result in immediate performance review, ticket deprioritization, or total contributor restructuring (revocation of commit access).
